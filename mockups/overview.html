Three ways to open the AI value chain tracker

Each direction uses the same nine real deals, checked on 31 Oct 2026 against company releases and one SEC filing. They are a sample to judge the look, not the full data. Color always means layer; fill pattern means confidence; dashed means a range or a cap.

A. Chain rail

Analyst feel. The eleven layers run left to right as a rail, then the largest flows sit below as bars on one scale. Best for "who gets the money?" in ten seconds.

B. Ledger

Editorial feel. One sentence states what the data says, then money out and money in by company, then the loops. Best for a reader who wants the story first.

Visual feel. Companies sit in their layer's column and money is drawn as bands, wider for bigger amounts, with loops traced in both directions. Best for seeing circular money at a glance.
